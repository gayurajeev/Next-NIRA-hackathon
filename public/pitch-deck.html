The Architecture

The Solution: Closed-Loop Civic Intelligence

NIRA bridges citizen smartphone cameras with rapid municipal field squads in four automated steps.

1

Snap & Analyze

Citizen takes a photo. Browser camera captures permanent Base64 image and runs instant AI heuristic analysis.

2

Ward Pinpoint

Ray-Casting Point-in-Polygon matches coordinates to Kochi Corporation wards and specific AE drainage officers.

3

Priority Triage

NIRA Priority Engine computes transparent 0–100 score factoring arterial traffic, schools, and flood hazard.

4

Verified Fix

Command center dispatches jetting crew. Squad uploads post-clearing photo proof before ticket closes.

AI Innovation

Computer Vision & Deterministic Prioritization

Transparent, explainable machine intelligence replacing arbitrary municipal guesswork.

Visual Blockage Classifier

Computer vision model evaluates blockage topology and surface pooling:

  • Categories: Blocked Storm Drain, Silt Accumulation, Broken Culvert, Garbage Dumping, Sewage Overflow.
  • Standing Water: Dry / Low, Detected, Severe Pooling, Submerged Channel.
  • Auto-Tuning: Suggests initial severity for instant citizen confirmation.

NIRA Priority Engine (0–100)

Deterministic algorithm weighing civic vulnerability:

  • Base Severity: Critical structural break (40 pts) vs trash (15 pts).
  • Corridor Bonus: Major arterial bus corridors (SA Road, MG Road) get +15 pts.
  • Cluster Density: Repeated nearby reports boost priority score automatically.
  • Strict SLAs: Score ≥ 70 triggers mandatory 3–4 hour squad dispatch.
Speaker Script (1:25 - 1:55): "Our dual-engine AI eliminates guesswork. The vision pipeline inspects the photo for silt and standing water, while our deterministic priority engine weights the score based on arterial traffic and school zone proximity. Everything is explainable with zero black-box ambiguity."
